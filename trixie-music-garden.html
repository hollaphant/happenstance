🦕 How to Play

🔒 Click a locked button → spell the word to unlock it
🎵 Click an unlocked button → turn the sound on or off
🎯 Double-click → solo that sound (mute all others)
⌨️ Keys 1–9 → toggle unlocked sounds
␣ Space → stop all sounds
🔊 Hover a locked button → hear a clue
🎵 Click the music button → pick music: major, minor, rain, traffic, or cafe
⭐ Spell with no mistakes → Perfect bonus!
🌱 Spell sounds → Garden flowers bloom!
🦕 Click the dino 5 times → secret surprise!

SECRET CODES (type on keyboard):
🔤 ABC → alphabet song   💃 DANCE → moonwalk
🦖 ROAR → big roar   🔢 COUNT → count to 10
🌈 COLORS → rainbow   🔷 SHAPES → shape lesson
👋 HELLO → say hello!
